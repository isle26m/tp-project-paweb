Tamanrasset

Informations

Wilaya
Tamanrasset is wilaya number 11, in southern Algeria inside the Ahaggar Mountains.
Altitude
The city lies at about 1,371 meters above sea level and has a hot desert climate with cold nights.
Culture
Tamanrasset is a Tuareg cultural center and gateway to Assekrem and Festival n’Ajjah.
